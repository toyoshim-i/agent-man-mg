# Live as instrument

## Question

What if visuals are played like an instrument, decided in the moment, instead of played back?

## Then

Club visuals began with slide projectors, film loops and video mixers: VJs cut and layered
material live to the DJ. Audiovisual acts sampled image and sound together, so a cut in the
video was also a cut in the music. Musicians and visual artists toured audiovisual concerts
where image and sound were generated from the same data in real time. Live coders projected
their code while writing it, so the audience watched the instrument being built as it played.

## On screen

- Decisions in time: changes land with the music because a person chose them, including
  near misses.
- Layering and mixing visible as a craft: two sources crossfading, a cut held back until the drop.
- The instrument visible: code, controllers or the performer's hands shown on screen.
- Risk: the set can go wrong, and that possibility is part of the energy.

Anchors: Coldcut's audiovisual sampling (1990s). Ryoji Ikeda and Carsten Nicolai's
audiovisual concerts. Live coding communities (algorave, Hydra).

## Now

Audio-reactive presets and generated visuals can run a whole night with no one at the
controls. Automatic visuals react but do not decide. Scarce: a human deciding visibly in the
moment; dialogue between the visual performer and the musician; the unrepeatable night.

## Openings

- Show the decision: the controller state, the cue list or the code as a quiet layer of the image.
- Duet: the visual performer answers the DJ (call and response), sometimes leading the change.
- Let the audience play one channel: a phone, a sensor, their noise drives one layer.
