# Lyrics as stage

## Question

What if the words of the song are the main visual, and the picture is the stage they perform on?

## Then

From 2007, people in Japan began releasing songs sung by voice synthesis software on a video
site where comments scroll over the picture. There was no singer to film and no budget: a
song came with one illustration and the lyrics. Synthesized vocals can be hard to follow,
and viewers sang along, so the lyrics had to be on screen, large. Over the 2010s the form
grew: the illustration was cut into parts and given small motions, and the lyrics became
designed motion typography with shapes, color flashes, RGB splits and camera shake on
accents. Producers, illustrators and video makers formed teams. By the late 2010s this
vocabulary had moved into mainstream Japanese pop videos.

## On screen

- Every line of the lyric is visible, placed as a composition element, timed to the voice word by word.
- Key words are marked by size, color, a break, or by being the only thing on screen.
- The illustration is a held stage: mostly still, with small loops (hair, blink, light).
- Type follows the vocal phrasing (long vowels stretch, breaths cut), not the drums.
- Two or three colors, often black, white and one strong hue; flat shapes; occasional glitch.

Anchors: ryo (supercell), "Melt" (2007). wowaka, "Rolling Girl" (2010). Jin, "Kagerou Daze"
(2011). YOASOBI, "Yoru ni Kakeru" (2019, video by Ai Nina).

## Now

Lyrics on screen are standard: automatic captions, lyric-video templates, and silent feed
viewing all show the words. Displaying lyrics is no longer an expression. Scarce: type that
interprets the words rather than repeating them; tension between what is sung and what is shown.

## Openings

- Show the subtext: the screen shows the word the singer avoids, while the voice sings around it.
- Breath-timed type: text appears and dissolves with the vocal envelope, not with the beat.
- Two layers of text: the sung lyric and a second voice (a reply, a translation, a thought)
  that disagrees with it.
