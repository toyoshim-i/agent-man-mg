# Ma

## Question

What if the empty interval, in time or in space, is the expression?

## Then

*Ma* is the Japanese idea of the interval: the pause between notes, the space between
stones in a garden, the stillness between movements in Noh theater. It is not absence; it is
where meaning gathers and the viewer completes the work. A film director placed shots of
empty rooms, streets and objects between scenes, so the audience rested and felt time pass.
Swiss graphic design, from another tradition, also treated white space as an active element.
Both trusted the viewer to fill the gap.

## On screen

- Large empty areas, with one element placed precisely.
- Pauses in time: the image holds, the sound stops, nothing moves, for long enough to be felt
  (1 to 3 seconds in a short piece is long).
- The gap between two events timed as carefully as the events.
- The viewer completes the meaning: what is not shown is implied by what is.

Anchors: Yasujiro Ozu's still shots between scenes. Noh theater. Japanese garden design.

## Now

Feeds punish emptiness: every second is filled to stop the scroll, and generated content
fills every space. Silence and space have become rare. Scarce: stillness; the confidence to
leave room; the viewer's own completion.

## Openings

- A short piece that is mostly empty time, where the one event lands with full weight.
- A VJ blackout or near-stillness as a statement in a long set, at the moment the room
  expects the most.
- Type set with *ma*: words separated by time rather than by space.
