# Now

Written October 2026. These are conditions, not trends to imitate. Each one made something
free and something scarce. New work lives on the scarce side. Revisit this page when a
condition changes; a stale "now" produces yesterday's ideas.

## The forces

| Force | Became free | Became scarce | Audiences now read |
|---|---|---|---|
| Generated images and video | polished, plausible pictures of anything, endlessly | intention, specificity, a point of view held across a whole piece | glossy smoothness as "maybe generated"; visible decisions, marks of the hand, and process as authorship |
| Templates and presets | competent kinetic type, beat-synced cuts, logo reveals | motion with a reason; a motion language that belongs to one subject | template moves as filler, scrolled past |
| Real-time GPU everywhere | simulation, particles, raymarching, in the browser and on phones | something to say with it; responsiveness that matters to the viewer | spectacle as baseline, not as content |
| Vertical, short, looped feeds | distribution | the first second; being remembered after the scroll; stillness and slowness as contrast | captions as default; loops as replays; sound-off viewing |
| Infinite sameness | volume | recognizability: a signature that holds across a series | a consistent world as a reason to follow |
| Remix and participation | stems, assets, re-edits; anything becomes material | work designed to be continued by others | a piece's value as what others build on it |
| Big screens in public | LED walls, facades, anamorphic billboards, concert stages | presence: being in the room, at the moment | the physical event as the original, the video as a souvenir |
| Nostalgia cycles | filters for VHS, CRT, early web, low-poly, Flash | the reason a past medium is used | the pre-generated internet as a sign of human making; cheap filters as costume |
| Live and streamed | broadcasting from anywhere | the unrepeatable moment; visuals that respond live | liveness as a value in itself |
| Data everywhere | charts, dashboards, data art presets | data with stakes, felt rather than displayed | visualization as decoration unless it means something |
| Cross-media stories | a song, its video, a novel, a character, all from small teams | a world coherent across media | characters and worlds as hubs, works as entrances |
| Safety and access norms | captions, flash limits, reduced motion are expected | - | ignoring them as careless |

## How to use it

In the [Concept](../process/2-concept.md) transposition table:

1. For the idea you picked, find the forces that touch its question. Usually one or two.
2. Copy "became free" into the table: the part of the old idea that is no longer an achievement.
3. Copy "became scarce": that is where the new piece must stand.
4. Check "audiences now read": your piece will be read through this, whether you intend it or not.

## A worked line

Idea [see-the-sound](see-the-sound.md): "how can image and sound be one score?" Then, sync
took months of hand work. Now real-time analysis makes sync free, and templates make
everything pulse. Scarce: deciding what *not* to sync; structure the ear cannot hear but the
eye can. Opening: one element listens, everything else holds still.
