# Ideas

Each page is one way of thinking that earlier makers found: the question it answered, the
conditions it came from, how it appeared on screen, and what is open about it today. Choose
by the question, not by the look. In the [Concept](../process/2-concept.md) step, pick three
from three different families.

## Sound and image

- [see-the-sound](see-the-sound.md): How can image and sound be one score?
- [instrument-as-actor](instrument-as-actor.md): How can a viewer see the structure of a piece of music?
- [cut-on-the-beat](cut-on-the-beat.md): What if editing itself is the rhythm section?

## Letters

- [title-as-essence](title-as-essence.md): How can an opening compress the whole work into a symbol?
- [type-as-image](type-as-image.md): What if letters are seen before they are read?
- [lyrics-as-stage](lyrics-as-stage.md): What if the words of a song are the main visual?

## Constraint and medium

- [constraint-as-style](constraint-as-style.md): How does a limitation become a signature?
- [stillness-as-motion](stillness-as-motion.md): How can holding still make motion stronger?
- [the-medium-shows](the-medium-shows.md): What if the medium's own flaws are the content?
- [math-is-the-image](math-is-the-image.md): What if a formula, made visible, is the picture?

## Rules and systems

- [identity-as-system](identity-as-system.md): How can something stay recognizable while it never looks the same?
- [rule-as-work](rule-as-work.md): What if the artwork is the rule, and each image one outcome?
- [data-sublime](data-sublime.md): How can something too large to grasp be felt?

## Body, hand and character

- [in-camera-truth](in-camera-truth.md): How can an image prove it really happened?
- [body-and-machine](body-and-machine.md): What happens when a body and a machine perform together?
- [character-as-vessel](character-as-vessel.md): How can a blank character carry everyone's stories?

## Screen, network and loop

- [the-screen-decides](the-screen-decides.md): How does where it is watched shape what it is?
- [work-as-node](work-as-node.md): What if a work is made to be continued by others?
- [loop-as-form](loop-as-form.md): What if a piece has no beginning and no end?

## Space and liveness

- [screen-dissolves](screen-dissolves.md): What if the image leaves the rectangle?
- [live-as-instrument](live-as-instrument.md): What if visuals are played, not played back?

## Time and meaning

- [transition-as-meaning](transition-as-meaning.md): What if the cut between two images says something?
- [world-across-media](world-across-media.md): How can one world live across song, video, story and character?
- [ma](ma.md): What if the empty interval is the expression?
