# The medium shows

## Question

What if the flaws and textures of the medium (scan lines, tape noise, compression blocks,
pixels) are not hidden but shown, and carry meaning?

## Then

Every medium has a signature failure: film scratches, video tape tracking errors, broadcast
interference, compressed video smearing when frames are dropped. Artists began provoking
these failures on purpose: corrupting files, bending circuits, deleting key frames so motion
from one shot drags the pixels of another. An opening could scratch and burn its own film to
show a disturbed mind. Each artifact pointed at the machine and made the viewer aware that
they were watching a recording, not a window.

## On screen

- Artifacts tied to a real mechanism: compression blocks align to a grid; tape errors move in
  horizontal bands; film damage is vertical and jumps between frames.
- Artifacts appear for a reason: a memory failing, a signal crossing a border, a system breaking.
- The clean image is shown first, so the failure is a failure.

## Now

Every artifact is a filter. VHS, CRT, film and glitch presets are free, and their textures now
read mainly as nostalgia: the internet before generated images, the era when an image was
made by someone. Generated images have their own new artifacts (melted hands, drifting
details, impossible text). Scarce: the reason; artifacts of today's medium rather than borrowed
ones from the 1990s.

## Openings

- Today's failures: frame interpolation smears, upscaler hallucinations, adaptive bitrate
  dropping quality mid-stream, a video call freezing on a face.
- Generation as the medium: an image that slowly forgets what it is, the way a generated video
  drifts. Shown on purpose, it is about memory or authenticity.
- A clean piece with exactly one failure, at the moment the story breaks.
