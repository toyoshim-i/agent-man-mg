# over-effected

## Symptom

You can name four or more effects at a glance. The subject is softened, split and noisy.
Random frames look like a mess. Squinting shows no clear big shapes.

## Cause

Effects were added to make it look better instead of fixing composition, value and timing.
Each one was tuned in isolation at a visible level.

## Fix

1. List every effect in the code. Delete any not in the design sheet.
2. Turn the rest off. Fix the still frame with [Hierarchy](../principle/hierarchy.md) and
   [Composition](../principle/composition.md) first.
3. Re-add effects one at a time at the [Finish](../principle/finish.md) defaults. Each must be
   missed when off and not nameable when on.
4. Glitch and heavy distortion only as bursts on accents ([Glitch](../recipe/glitch.md)).
