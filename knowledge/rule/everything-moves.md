# everything-moves

## Symptom

Several elements move at the same time with similar speed and size. The viewer's eye jumps
around; after a few seconds it is tiring. Freeze frames look fine, playback looks like noise.

## Cause

No primary event per moment. All elements were animated from t = 0, or ambient motion is as
strong as the main action.

## Fix

1. In the timing chart, mark one primary event per 0.25 s window. Move all others out of
   that window or make them secondary.
2. Secondary motion: 10 to 30% of the primary amplitude, starting 2 to 6 frames later
   ([Overlap](../principle/overlap.md)).
3. Ambient drift: below 30% of the star's speed.
4. Hand off focus: the old star settles before the new star moves
   ([Hierarchy](../principle/hierarchy.md)).
