# monotony

## Symptom

Every second looks like every other. Nothing lands. Plotting frame difference per second gives
a flat line. A loop has no event in 8 bars.

## Cause

No energy curve was planned; one speed and density throughout; peaks approached without a
breath; everything pulses on every beat so nothing is an accent.

## Fix

1. Write an energy curve with one maximum ([Energy](../principle/energy.md)).
2. Vary at least two dials between sections: speed, density, brightness, contrast.
3. Put a breath (stillness or darkness, 2 to 8 frames or 1 to 2 beats) before the peak.
4. Reduce beat pulses to one element class; move other changes to bars and phrases.
5. Loops: add one event every 4 to 8 bars.
