# strobe-risk

## Symptom

Mean brightness of the frame swings by more than 10% more than three times in a second.
Full-frame white flashes or inversions on fast beats. Red flashes. Large scrolling stripes or
checkerboards at high contrast.

## Cause

Brightness or inversion mapped to every kick or 1/8 note; glitch bursts that invert; audio
reaction on a full-frame layer.

## Fix

1. Route every large-area flash through a limiter of 3 per second
   ([Flash safety](../vj/vj-safety.md)).
2. Keep kick-driven brightness swings below 10% of maximum, or confine them to under 25% of
   the screen.
3. Remove red flashes entirely.
4. Lower stripe contrast below 0.5 L or stop the stripes moving.
5. Re-run the flash count check in [Review](../process/6-review.md).
