# static-composition

## Symptom

Everything is centered, or spread evenly over the frame. No negative space. Squinting shows
either one gray blur or a uniform texture. The frame looks like a default template.

## Cause

Placement without a grid or a focal decision; equal sizes; margins not designed.

## Fix

1. Reduce to 2 to 4 masses. Put the star on a thirds intersection
   ([Composition](../principle/composition.md)).
2. Make sizes differ by a ratio of 1.5 to 2.
3. Leave at least 20% negative space, 40% for minimal looks, on one side.
4. Let one element bleed off the frame edge by 20% or more for tension.
5. Align edges to a grid.
